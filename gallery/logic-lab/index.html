How to use it
  • Mouse: drag parts to move them. Click a switch to flip it. Click a part's right pin, then a left pin on another part, to draw a wire. Click a wire to delete it.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a part. Arrow keys move it (Shift for big steps). Space flips a switch. W starts a wire from the focused part. Then Tab to the target and press 1 or 2 for its input pin. X unplugs a part's inputs. Delete removes it. Esc cancels a wire.
  • Your board is saved in this browser automatically.
